Run from scratch (estimate → adjust → fuse w0/w3 → GT eval) per scene-level.

**Decision anchor: ETH3D (deterministic) — BlendedMVS fusion nondeterminism now FIXED (2026-07-08,
commit 763be0d).** When this report's [2] fusion decisions were made, BlendedMVS fusion was
nondeterministic and its rows were supplementary context only — so all fusion conclusions below are
drawn from the 18 deterministic ETH3D scene-levels. The cause was NOT the "OpenMP race" it was long
attributed to: it was a time-seeded RNG in `Scene::EstimateNeighborViewsPointCloud` (the empty-point-
cloud neighbor-covisibility estimate, which ran on every BlendedMVS densify because InterfaceMVSNet
imports no sparse cloud). That is now fixed (deterministic hash), and BlendedMVS fusion is byte-stable
across runs — its rows are being re-fused deterministically and promoted to first-class; the w2/w3
decision in [2] will be re-checked with trustworthy BlendedMVS data. (BlendedMVS conf-ROC rows were
never affected and hold as a holdout supplement; see `AGGREGATE_FINAL.md`.)
