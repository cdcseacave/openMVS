# Pipeline overview

OpenMVS turns images into a textured mesh in two programs: `CreateStructure` (Structure-from-Motion, `libs/SFM`) recovers the camera poses and a sparse point cloud, and the MVS applications (`libs/MVS`) densify, mesh, refine and texture it. This page names the stages in order and points to the document that describes each. The stage-by-stage reference of the SfM pipeline (order, options, which options combine, saved-scene resume) is [design/SfMPipeline.md](design/SfMPipeline.md); nothing of it is repeated here.

## Structure-from-Motion (`CreateStructure`)

1. **Import and cameras.** The images (a folder, a list, or a saved `.sfm` scene) are read with their EXIF data; identical cameras are merged into one shared camera, and poses, a focal length or distortion given on the command line are applied. A pinhole camera or a spherical (equirectangular) one is chosen per scene. See [design/ImportAndCameras.md](design/ImportAndCameras.md) and [design/SphericalCameras.md](design/SphericalCameras.md).
2. **Features.** Keypoints and descriptors are extracted per image (SiftGPU by default, AKAZE, ORB and SIFT on the CPU). See [design/FeatureExtraction.md](design/FeatureExtraction.md) and [design/SiftGPUFeatureExtraction.md](design/SiftGPUFeatureExtraction.md).
3. **Pair selection.** The pairs worth matching are proposed from a vocabulary tree, from global descriptors of the dense matcher (retrieval), from known poses, or exhaustively, with a sequential prior and a second round fed by the verified matches. See [design/PairSelection.md](design/PairSelection.md).
4. **Matching.** Each pair is matched by its descriptors, or, with a dense matcher (RoMa v2 or FlexGlue, run in-process through ONNX Runtime), by one dense pass that judges the pair on a bidirectional warp, matches the described keypoints guided by it and fills the overlap with dense correspondences. See [design/PairSelection.md](design/PairSelection.md) and [design/DenseMatching.md](design/DenseMatching.md); how to obtain the models: [DenseMatcherModels.md](DenseMatcherModels.md).
5. **Pair verification, lens fit and focal calibration.** Every pair is verified by a robust two-view geometry that yields its inlier matches and a relative pose; the radial lens of cameras without one is fitted and the focals of cameras that do not trust theirs are calibrated on the whole graph. See [design/TwoViewVerificationAndLens.md](design/TwoViewVerificationAndLens.md).
6. **Pair weighting.** Each verified pair gets one composite weight, ranking its reliability; the weight and its bars are read by the tracks, the clustering and the cut. See [design/PairWeight.md](design/PairWeight.md).
7. **View-graph filtering.** Every pair is scored against the triangles it belongs to, then a contradiction cut removes (or repairs) the pairs that a robust averaging of rotations and baseline directions contradicts: the pairs a look-alike or repeated structure fabricated. See [design/TripletScores.md](design/TripletScores.md) and [design/ContradictionCut.md](design/ContradictionCut.md).
8. **Reconstruction.** With poses imported, the known-poses route triangulates and adjusts around them. Otherwise the hierarchical route (the default) clusters the images into blocks of bounded size, reconstructs each block incrementally and in parallel (tracks, a star initializer, resection, bundle adjustment), resects the images no block holds against the block they connect to best, and merges the blocks into one model in rounds by a robust averaging over the block graph. See [design/HierarchicalSFM.md](design/HierarchicalSFM.md), [design/TrackBuilding.md](design/TrackBuilding.md), [design/IncrementalReconstruction.md](design/IncrementalReconstruction.md) and [design/BundleAdjustment.md](design/BundleAdjustment.md).
9. **Final adjustment and leftover images.** The tracks are filtered and triangulated again, the whole scene is adjusted once, weakly connected images are filtered, and every image still unregistered is tried again against the finished model. See [design/BundleAdjustment.md](design/BundleAdjustment.md) and [design/IncrementalReconstruction.md](design/IncrementalReconstruction.md).
10. **Alignment, pose quality and export.** The result is aligned to the imported poses or to GPS, the per-image pose covariance can be estimated, and the scene is saved as `.sfm`; poses and pose quality can be exported as CSV and the scene as an `.mvs` for the MVS stage: distorted cameras as Brown cameras of the original images, which the MVS stage undistorts when it loads them (or as undistorted image copies with `--undistort-extension`), and spherical cameras as native spherical cameras (or as rendered cube-map faces with `--spherical-export cubemap`). See [design/PoseUncertainty.md](design/PoseUncertainty.md), [design/SfMPipeline.md](design/SfMPipeline.md) and [design/SphericalCameras.md](design/SphericalCameras.md).

## Multi-View Stereo

The MVS stages read an `.mvs` scene (from `CreateStructure --export-mvs` or from an interface application: `InterfaceCOLMAP`, `InterfaceOpenMVG`, `InterfaceMetashape`, `InterfaceMVSNet`, `InterfacePolycam`) and each is its own application.

1. **`DensifyPointCloud`.** Depth maps are estimated per image against its neighbour views (PatchMatch by default, or semi-global matching with `--fusion-mode -2`), their confidence recalibrated (`ADJUST_CONFIDENCE`, automatic by default), and fused into one point cloud whose points carry the views that saw them. See [design/SemiGlobalMatching.md](design/SemiGlobalMatching.md), [design/DepthMapConfidence.md](design/DepthMapConfidence.md) and [design/DepthMapFusion.md](design/DepthMapFusion.md).
2. **`ReconstructMesh`.** A watertight mesh is extracted from the point cloud by a Delaunay tetrahedralization, a visibility energy and an s-t min-cut, then cleaned. See [design/DelaunayMeshReconstruction.md](design/DelaunayMeshReconstruction.md).
3. **`RefineMesh`.** The mesh is subdivided and moved toward photo-consistency under a smoothness term (CPU and CUDA paths). See [design/MeshRefinement.md](design/MeshRefinement.md).
4. **`TextureMesh`.** Each face is assigned the image that should texture it, the seams are levelled in colour, and the regions are packed into one or more atlases. See [design/MeshTexturing.md](design/MeshTexturing.md).

Other applications: `ExtractKeyframes` (keyframes from a video), `TransformScene` (apply a transformation to a scene), `Viewer` (interactive visualization of any stage), `Tests`.

Notes: [notes/BlockGraphMerge.md](notes/BlockGraphMerge.md) keeps a merge design that was measured and not shipped, so that it can be rebuilt.
